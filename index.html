Automatic Airsoft Video Machine

Your airsoft game, with your heart rate, route and story on the video.

Drop in your helmet-cam clips and your watch's data. It puts a live HUD on the video (heart rate, map, speed, shots), lets you cut on a multitrack timeline, add story and game cards, and exports a finished movie.

Windows 0.4.0

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Nothing to install: .NET and FFmpeg are inside the folder.

Download for Windows 208 MB
How to start it
  1. Unzip the download (right-click › Extract All).
  2. Open the folder and double-click AVM.exe.
  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info › Run anyway (the app isn't signed).
Your projects and exports go to Documents\AVM.

Mac 1.6.46

macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, Apple silicon (M1 or newer).

Download for Mac 11 MB
How to start it
  1. Unzip it and drag AVM Swift to Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click the app › Open › Open. If macOS still refuses, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
The app isn't signed by Apple, so macOS asks once. If it says the app is "damaged", run this once in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AVM Swift.app"

Projects are the same on both: a project made on a Mac opens on Windows and the other way round. The older Python version for Windows (3.x) is still under All versions.

What it does

Live HUDHeart rate, map with your route, speed, distance, clock and shots, in several themes.
Watch dataApple Health export, GPX or TCX from Garmin, Polar, Suunto, Strava…
Friends' anglesTheir videos on Video 2, lined up by clock or by sound; their dots on your map.
CardsStory, war, funny and game cards, titles, generators and motion cards with you cut out of the video.
Multitrack editorCut, trim, move, magnetic edges, music, undo; live preview with sound.
Export720p to 4K using your graphics card's encoder when there is one.
Shots and hitsYour shots found from the sound and counted from the magazine; hits flatline the heartbeat.
Game styles40+ looks, each with its own HUD, menus, titles, transitions and shot marks.
EffectsLooks, LUTs, cinema look, camera effects, slow motion, replays, and smoke, fire and burning logo transitions.
SubtitlesSpeech to text and English subtitles made on your own computer (Mac); friends' lines in their colours.
Field plannerDraw the field, bases and routes on a satellite map; briefing maps and a 3D flyover from it.
kuulaportti.fiThe day's game, players, factions, history and weather found by themselves.
Tech cardsGun teardown, chronograph, HPA and kit cards, drawn for your own gear.
Narration and musicText-to-speech narration, songs, beat edits and music videos; the game sound ducks under the voice.
Media libraryAll your videos from folders, drives and NAS, by day, event or on a map, with smooth-play copies.
Reels and YouTubeVertical 9:16 Reels that follow the action, short clips, thumbnails and YouTube chapters (Mac).
